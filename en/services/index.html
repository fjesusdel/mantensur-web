Common areas, offices and premises

Building cleaning

Clean buildings and common areas are essential for a pleasant and safe environment. That is why we offer a complete service designed to cover every need of your building and its shared spaces.

We adapt the plan to each building and its residents, with the frequency and schedule that suit you best, and we use quality products and equipment so the result shows after every visit.

What it includes

  • Entrance halls, stairs, landings and lifts.
  • Windows, doors and railings.
  • Garages, storage rooms and common areas.
  • Offices and premises.
  • Waste removal and surface disinfection.
Car parks and garages of residential communities

Complete car park cleaning

A clean car park gives your community a good image and is safer for the people who use it. We take care of the deep cleaning of car parks and garages with professional machinery.

For the floors we use a mechanised scrubber-dryer, which leaves the surface clean and dry in a single pass.

We adapt the frequency and schedule to your community to disturb residents as little as possible.

What it includes

  • Floor scrubbing with a mechanised scrubber-dryer.
  • Removal of oil stains and tyre marks.
  • Cleaning of ramps, entrances and walkways.
  • Cleaning of walls, columns and drain grates.
  • Removal of waste and built-up dirt.
Gardens and green areas

Gardening

A well-kept garden improves the look of your community and creates a welcoming, relaxing atmosphere. We look after it all year round so it always looks its best.

We work with you to adapt the garden to the style of your community, using environmentally friendly gardening practices.

What it includes

  • Lawn mowing and care.
  • Pruning of hedges, shrubs and trees.
  • Watering and irrigation system checks.
  • Pest control and fertilising.
  • Design and planting of green areas.
Clean water all season

Pool maintenance

Keeping a pool in perfect condition takes time, consistency and know-how. We take care of everything so your community can enjoy it without worries.

We design a maintenance plan suited to your pool, its use and your budget, and we advise you so the water is always in good condition.

What it includes

  • Cleaning of the pool shell, floor and surface.
  • Water treatment and testing.
  • Checks of the filter and equipment.
  • Opening at the start of the season.
  • Closing and winterising at the end.
