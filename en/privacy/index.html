3. Legal basis

The legal basis is your consent, given when you send the form or write to us (art. 6.1.a GDPR) and, if you hire our services, the performance of the contract (art. 6.1.b GDPR). You may withdraw your consent at any time.

We keep enquiry data for as long as needed to answer it and, at most, one year if no service is hired. If a service is hired, the data is kept for the duration of the relationship and then for the periods required by law.
