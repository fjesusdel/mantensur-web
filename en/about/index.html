Who we are

Experience in comprehensive maintenance

At Mantensur we have spent over 10 years in the comprehensive maintenance of residential communities, buildings and homes.

We offer a wide range of services that we group according to the needs of each community and client, so the service is always tailored.

From the daily cleaning of common areas to the care of gardens and pools, we take care of every detail so you can enjoy a clean, well-kept environment at all times.

Our goal: a quality service that keeps your community looking its best.

How we work

Dedication and attention to detail

We work with dedication so that every corner of your community is spotless, contributing to everyone's well-being and comfort.

Regular, effective maintenance preserves the value of the building and prevents larger, more costly problems later on.

Where we work

Córdoba and its province
